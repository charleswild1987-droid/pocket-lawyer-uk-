Google Play Store & UK GDPR Compliant

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 2026 | App Name: PocketLawyer UK

PocketLawyer UK ("we", "our", or "us") provides a legal rights reference companion, dispute letter generator, legal forms generator, and landlord/commercial agreements suite designed for individuals and businesses operating in the United Kingdom (England & Wales).

1. Data Collection & Zero-Tracker Architecture

PocketLawyer UK operates with a privacy-first, on-device architecture:

  • No Remote Tracking or Analytics: We do not track your location, browsing habits, or sell personal information to third parties or advertising networks.
  • Local Storage: All user inputs—including saved contracts, letters before action, tenancy agreements, incident logs, and audio notes—are stored exclusively in your device's secure browser LocalStorage / IndexedDB sandbox.
  • Audio Recordings: Incident audio recorded during police stop-and-search or custody encounters is processed and held exclusively in device memory and downloadable directly to your phone's file storage. It is never uploaded to any external server.

2. Google Play Billing & Subscriptions

PocketLawyer UK offers a 7-day free trial, followed by a £2.99 per month subscription (inclusive of 20% UK VAT):

  • Billing Processing: When purchased via Android, payments and recurring subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or process your credit/debit card numbers directly.
  • Managing & Cancelling: You can manage, pause, or cancel your subscription at any time directly through Google Play by navigating to: Google Play Store > Profile Icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Trial Terms: If you cancel during the 7-day free trial, you will not be charged.

3. UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018 Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, you possess rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Erasure (Google Play Requirement): You can wipe all stored drafts, incident recordings, and cached preferences at any time using the in-app "Data Safety & Device Storage Wipe" button in Settings.
  • Right of Access & Portability: You may export all generated contracts, statutory letters, and incidents in plain text, PDF, or printable formats directly from the app at zero cost.

4. Legal Disclaimer

PocketLawyer UK is an educational information resource and automated template generator for UK legal principles (governed by the laws of England and Wales). It does not provide regulated legal advice or substitute for consultation with a qualified Solicitor or Barrister regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) or Bar Standards Board (BSB).
